Workshop 2027: Theory and Applications of (∞,n)-Categories

This workshop aims to introduce students to the theory of (∞,n)-categories and its applications in modern mathematics. To this end, the workshop will feature four lecture series, each accompanied by exercise sessions and discussions of open problems:

Lecture A(∞,n)-Categorical definitions and constructions in modelsViktoriya Ozornova (MPIM Bonn, Germany)
Lecture BModel-independent (∞,n)-category theoryLorenzo Riva (Harvard CMSA, US)
Lecture CHigher categories in categorical representation theoryJulia Plavnik (Indiana University, US)
Lecture DTarget higher categories for TQFTsTheo Johnson-Freyd (Dalhousie University, Canada)

Date and place

The workshop will take place from Sunday, July 4 to Friday, July 9, 2027 at BIRS-IAN at UBC Okanagan campus in Kelowna, British Columbia, Canada.
